# v0.21 rationale — one session per pane, as the rows show it (sprint 373)

*Locked 2026-11-08. Supersedes `0.20.json`; 88 tags, 87 live. Earlier rationales cover everything not named here.*

## The change

**`P-0.21-S5-REPLACES`** (`INVARIANT_PROPOSED`, Layer 5). v0.20's S5 read: "after SESSION_OPEN_ACKED or SESSION_ATTACH_STARTED for a pane, no second one for that pane until SESSION_ENDED_LOCAL or PANE_CLOSED". The records page attaches another session into a pane that holds one (`attachExisting`), by design, and the runtime checker broke the rule on three normal gate runs: `view_signals.ts`, `tool_output_toggle.ts`, `both_session_shapes.ts`. The rule's point, one session per pane at a time, is now stated as what the pane's records can show:
- a pane's last ack or attach names its session, replacing any before it, until PANE_CLOSED;
- every later TRANSCRIPT_ROW_DRAWN or TRANSCRIPT_ROW_SKIPPED of the pane that names a session names that one.

A row of the session the pane held, drawn after the switch, breaks it.
