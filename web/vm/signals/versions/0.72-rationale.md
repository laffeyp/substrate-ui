# v0.72 rationale — Markdown rendered, links followed

*2026-11-14, sprint 503. Bumps v0.71 to v0.72 (159 tags). Two proposals: P-0.72-MARKDOWN-SHOWN, P-0.72-LINK-FOLLOWED.*

## Layer 1 — Lexical

`EDITOR_MARKDOWN_SHOWN` (form) marks a tab's switch between its rendered form and its source, from the tab's menu. `EDITOR_LINK_FOLLOWED` (kind, view, found) marks a link followed: ⌘-click in the code, a click in the rendered view. Both are framed by the `editor_run` session and fall under S11.

## Layer 2 — Payload

`kind` is external, file or heading; `view` source or rendered; `found` false when the target was not there. The address and the path stay out of the trace (E1). `EDITOR_FILE_OPENED.how` gains `link`.
