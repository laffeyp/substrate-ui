# v0.52 rationale — how a divider drag ended

*2026-11-10, sprint 448. Bumps v0.51 to v0.52 (149 tags). One proposal: P-0.52-RESIZE-ENDED. Grounds: one `editor_resize` run on the bundle whose right-column drag got nine of its ten moves and no pointerup, and so wrote no EDITOR_COLUMN_RESIZED (BLACKBOARD 2026-11-10, open question).*

## Layer 2 — Payload

EDITOR_COLUMN_RESIZED gains `ended`. `pointerup`: the button was released over the captured handle. `cancelled`: the browser sent pointercancel. `blurred`: the capture ended with the window out of focus. `capture_lost`: it ended otherwise; the handle is still in the page then, since a removed capture target's lostpointercapture goes to the document and not to the handle (Pointer Events, W3C). A drag now ends either way, and the trace says which, so the next lost pointerup is named rather than silent.

## Dual-contract pairing

| Behaviour | View-side counterpart |
|---|---|
| A drag ended by its pointerup | EDITOR_COLUMN_RESIZED ended pointerup; the column at the drag's size |
| A drag whose capture was lost | EDITOR_COLUMN_RESIZED ended capture_lost; the column at the size the drag had reached, and no change on a later hover |
