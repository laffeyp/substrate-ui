## What v0.7.1 lands

### TAG_SPLIT_LANDED — PANE_SWITCHED ⇒ PANE_SWITCHED + VIEW_SWITCHED

The v0.7 lock quietly extended `PANE_SWITCHED.to_pane` from four values
(`run | topology | scene | io`) to six (`... | terminal | desktop`) at
the grader by adding `VIEW_SCOPE_TO_PANE`. One tag carried two grading
contracts: inner-pane switches paired with a `*_RENDERED` paint;
view-scope container flips carried no paint pairing.

That is tag polymorphism, and it violates commitment 1 (`grammar/PRINCIPLES.md`:
"the vocabulary is the contract, refactored like a public API"). One tag
means one thing.

v0.7.1 splits: `PANE_SWITCHED` returns to its four-value inner-pane
contract; `VIEW_SWITCHED{to_view, prior_view, subject_record}` takes
the two-value view-scope contract. Each has one grading rule.

### VIEW_SWITCHED

- **Category:** `pane`. **Stratum:** `event`.
- **Payload:** `to_view, prior_view, subject_record`.
- **Closed set for `to_view` + `prior_view`:** `desktop | terminal`.
  Enumerated in the tag's `note`; parity gate reads the note.
- **Pairing invariant (new, added to v0.7.1's invariants block):**
  every `VIEW_SWITCHED{to_view:desktop}` is followed within 500ms by
  exactly one of `GRAPH_RENDERED / TOPOLOGY_RENDERED / SCENE_RENDERED /
  IO_RENDERED` with matching `subject_record`. `VIEW_SWITCHED{to_view:terminal}`
  carries no downstream pane-render pairing (the terminal view is empty
  until sprint 035; sprint 035 adds a `TERMINAL_RENDERED` tag or
  equivalent and folds the terminal side into the pairing invariant).

### PANE_SWITCHED note text

Reverted to its v0.6 four-value shape, with a line naming that view-scope
flips moved to `VIEW_SWITCHED` under the TAG_SPLIT. Closed set now
enumerated in the note (H2 fix).
