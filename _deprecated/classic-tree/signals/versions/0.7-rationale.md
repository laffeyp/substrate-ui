## What v0.7 adds

Five new tags in the existing `driver_session` category, and one
optional payload field on `RECORDS_LOADED`. Every addition motivates
a piece-G sprint.

### DRIVER_PATCHED

- **Category:** `driver_session`. **Stratum:** `event`.
- **Payload:** `session_id, driver, prior_driver`.
- **Motivating sprint:** 036a (driver picker).
- **Fires:** on successful `PATCH /api/session/<id> {driver}`
  round-trip. `prior_driver` is the driver at emit time; equals the
  effect of the CLI `/model` slash.

### BUNDLE_ATTACHED

- **Category:** `driver_session`. **Stratum:** `event`.
- **Payload:** `session_id, bundle`. **Optional:** `prior_bundle`.
- **Motivating sprint:** 036b (bundle picker).
- **Fires:** at create-time (`POST /api/session {bundle}`) and
  mid-session (`PATCH /api/session/<id> {bundle}`, gated on substrate-ui
  sprint 032b). Mid-session attach re-assembles the seed and lands a
  substrate `TranscriptCompacted{reason:"bundle_changed"}` envelope on
  the record. `prior_bundle` is null on create-time.

### WORKSPACE_SELECTED

- **Category:** `driver_session`. **Stratum:** `event`.
- **Payload:** `session_id, workspace, workspace_shape`.
- **Motivating sprint:** 036c (workspace picker).
- **Fires:** at create-time only. Workspace is immutable per session;
  the daemon refuses PATCH on `workspace`. `workspace_shape` is one of
  `flat | worktree | isolate`.

### TOOLS_RESTRICTED

- **Category:** `driver_session`. **Stratum:** `event`.
- **Payload:** `session_id, tools`.
- **Motivating sprint:** 036d (tools drawer).
- **Fires:** on `PATCH /api/session/<id> {tools}`. `tools` is a
  lexicographically sorted string array; empty means no restriction.
  Matches the CLI `/tools` slash effect.

### ISOLATE_TOGGLED

- **Category:** `driver_session`. **Stratum:** `event`.
- **Payload:** `session_id, isolate`.
- **Motivating sprint:** 036e (isolate toggle).
- **Fires:** at create-time only, and only when the user explicitly
  sets the toggle. Default-off untouched does not emit. Mutually
  exclusive with `workspace_shape=worktree` at the daemon (server
  returns 400).

### RECORDS_LOADED.bucket (optional payload field)

- **Motivating sprint:** 034b (four-bucket rail).
- **Value set:** `sessions | recent | bundles | records`.
- **Fires:** one `RECORDS_LOADED` per bucket after the rail renders.

## What v0.7 does not add

No new invariants. The `checkSessionBookends` block already covers the
driver-session pairing at the substrate wire level; the five new tags
are UI-side witnesses of a daemon round-trip and pair by construction
with the manifest state their PATCH/POST writes. Cross-checking is
handled by the per-control parity smoke (036a-e) and the consolidated
`test_ui_control_parity.py` (036f).

No new categories. Every addition sits inside the existing
`driver_session` category and the existing `records` category
(RECORDS_LOADED extension).

No changes to the browser-side lifecycle tags. `SESSION_INIT` and
`SESSION_ENDED` remain the browser tab-load/unload pair, unchanged
since v0.5.
