## What v0.7.2 lands

### DRIVER_PARAMS_PATCHED

- **Category:** `driver_session`. **Stratum:** `event`.
- **Payload:** `session_id, params, prior_params`. Each dict may
  carry any subset of `think` (bool), `max_tokens` (int ≥ 0; 0 =
  uncapped), `timeout` (float > 0), `num_ctx` (int ≥ 1). Absent keys
  mean "responder default"; empty dict means "no per-session override."
- **Motivating sprints:** substrate-ui 032c (SessionManifest gains
  `driver_params` field; `_daemon_driver_resolver(name, params)`
  extended; `_session_patch` lifts `driver_params` into `_PATCHABLE`);
  substrate-ui 035v (terminal-view params drawer + `/set` slash that
  fires the PATCH and this tag on the ACK).
- **Fires:** UI-side after a successful `PATCH /api/session/<id>
  {driver_params}` round-trip. Not fired from the substrate record
  (this is a client-daemon control-plane event; the record does not
  need a corresponding envelope — `OllamaResponder`'s next
  construction reflects the change, observable in `SessionStarted`
  on the next resumed turn if any).
- **Pairing invariant (implicit, no formal check):** every
  `DRIVER_PARAMS_PATCHED{session_id, params}` reflects a manifest
  state readable via `GET /api/session/<id>` at emit time. The
  round-trip is the observation; no downstream record envelope is
  contracted.

## Why a new tag, not a `DRIVER_PATCHED` extension

Grammar/PRINCIPLES commitment 1: "vocabulary is the contract,
refactored like a public API; one tag means one thing." Extending
`DRIVER_PATCHED` with an optional `params` payload field would
overload the tag: the same name would signal "driver swapped" OR
"params changed" OR "both." Consumers would need to inspect payload
shape to know which happened.

That is exactly the shape REVIEW-2026-08-28-piece-g-work-so-far AP5
caught with `PANE_SWITCHED` (which briefly served both inner-pane
switches and view-scope container flips in v0.7); the disciplined fix
was TAG_SPLIT in v0.7.1 (introduced `VIEW_SWITCHED` for the second
contract). Same reasoning applies here: driver-swap and driver-params-
change are two distinct semantic events; two tags.

## What v0.7.2 does not change

- No invariant additions. `DRIVER_PARAMS_PATCHED` is a client-daemon
  control-plane event; the record has no counterpart to pair against.
  The parity smoke lives in the sprint test
  (`test_server_session_driver_params.py`) instead of a vocab
  invariant.
- No changes to any other tag's schema or note.
- No changes to categories, entity_merges, runtime_operators,
  external_vocabulary_ref, or payload_types.
- No changes to invariants block.
- The five v0.7 session-control tags (DRIVER_PATCHED, BUNDLE_ATTACHED,
  WORKSPACE_SELECTED, TOOLS_RESTRICTED, ISOLATE_TOGGLED) and the
  RECORDS_LOADED.bucket field carry over unchanged.
