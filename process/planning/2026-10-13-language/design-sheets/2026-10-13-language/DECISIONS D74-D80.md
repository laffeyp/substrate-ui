# Language measurements — design decisions D74–D80

- **D80 · 2026-10-13 — Stay with the doc; no added complication.** The language frames follow DESIGN language-in-the-app r2 as written, plus only the Architect's directed changes (named settings, arrows after their setting). Two proposals were considered and NOT adopted, because neither the doc nor the code carries them: colouring shifts by whether they agree with the setting (needs an intent table that does not exist), and marking guidance "(from model)". Corrected to the kernel vocabulary: a finished turn's recap reads "returned (replied)" (session v0.11 Returned + Returned.reason), not "parked (replied)". Data status: the drawn series are synthetic but detector-true (exact PageHinkley port); no recorded session with LanguageProfile events was found in the attached folder (substrate/process/runs searched), so real-record frames wait on one being supplied.

In narrow panes the settings line wraps under the turn recap rather than clipping.
- **D78 · 2026-10-13 — Dots are dead.** The number-and-dot cell page (old 22a) is removed from the sheet; the language page exists only as turn 23's three views (ribbons · bars · deviation). 22c (short session) is redrawn in the bars view — each reply's own bar, no band, no marks before the 20-reply warm-up; 22d (explanations) is redrawn in the ribbons view with the readout row. Turn 22 now holds only the bar-line candidates (22b/22e/22f) and those two frames.
- **D77 · 2026-10-13 — Detector-true data; named settings; plain copy.** (a) Every ▲ on turns 22–23 is placed where an exact JS port of substrate.language.drift.PageHinkley (DEFAULT_DRIFT) fires on the full t0–t33 series (tools/lang-data.js): words at t22, hedges at t29; no other measure alarms. Bands, deviation SDs and the selected turn (t29) use the detector's own 20-reply warm-up. (b) The bar line names its settings — guidance · length · tone — in three candidate forms: 22b A (shift written in words after its setting: "length full words up · tone blunt hedges up"), 22e B (the setting carries ↑/↓, the measure is in the hover), 22f C (settings on their own row, one fixed slot each). Narrow panes use the B form on a second line. (c) Hover copy is plain and true: "Hedges are up. Flagged at turn 29. Tone is set to blunt, so fewer were expected." (the turn the detector fired, not a claim about when the rise began) — no statistics in the copy; the numbers live on the page. Short sessions: "Shift marks start after 20 replies. This session has 2." (the detector's warm-up gates marks and the band only; trend lines have their own text-length minimums).
- **D76 · 2026-10-13 — Indicator attaches to its setting; all three stream views ship.** (a) The bar line's tail is guidance · length · tone (e.g. "high · full · blunt"; Architect-confirmed order). Each LanguageDrift now sits directly after the setting meant to move its measure, as "measure ↑/↓" in that group's colour with a dotted underline — "… · high · full words ↑ · blunt hedges ↑" — replacing the opaque "N shifts" count; hover/focus opens ONE shift at a time (group · setting · measure, two sentences against the warm-up, the setting's intent). (b) 23a ribbons, 23b bars, 23c deviation all ship behind one segmented toggle on the language lens row (same pattern as D17's graph-direction toggle). (c) Data correction: the 23 frames' sentence-length and list series no longer jump after the length change — only the two marked shifts (words, hedges) are present, so the drawings and the drift marks agree. 22a and 22d were regenerated with the same corrected series, so every frame on the sheet shows only the two marked shifts. - **D75 · 2026-10-13 — Language as a stream (turn 23, open: pick one).** Turn 22's number-plus-dot cells with thin window lines rejected as hard to read. Kept: time down, 18px rows aligned with the graph, turn = linked referent, change rule, drift mark, group colours. Changed: the cell becomes a drawing on a per-measure LIFELINE (D30's sequence-diagram theory carried over: measures are lifelines, turns deposit onto them), and numbers move to ONE readout row for the selected turn (Few). Three candidates on the same data: 23a ribbons (ThemeRiver silhouette per lane, width = trailing window, per-turn hairline strata; Havre et al. 2000, Byron & Wattenberg 2008); 23b activation bars off a common baseline with the window as a curve at the bar ends (the graph's own vocabulary; Cleveland & McGill length encoding); 23c deviation stream (Shewhart chart folded into a stream, every lane in warm-up SDs — the unit drift.Alarm.statistic already reports — two-tone emphasis past ±2 SD). Grounded: lanes = drift.WATCHED (13), warm-up = 20 (DEFAULT_DRIFT); the language lens does not yet exist in reveal.html, so the chosen frame becomes the build spec.
- **D74 · 2026-10-13 — Language measurements (turn 22, from DESIGN language-in-the-app r2; bar-line format verified against reveal.html activity strip).** (a) A fifth meta lens, LANGUAGE, after structure: stream+graph · i/o · structure · language · scene. Rows = turns, time DOWN, 18px rows aligned with the graph; selection is the seq, linked both ways with stream+graph. Thirteen default columns (drift.WATCHED) grouped LENGTH · TONE · GUIDANCE by the setting meant to move them (group colours: length #82a5c8, tone #7fb3b8, guidance #c9a0b8). Each cell: the value as a number (zero in the dimmest grey) + a dot on the column's own scale (unit + range printed under the header); the trailing-window value as a thin group-coloured line; the warm-up mean ± 1 SD as a faint band once 20 values exist; SessionChanged as a full-width grey rule labelled "length normal → full · turn N"; LanguageDrift as ▲/▼ in the cell, value and dot taking the group colour. Streams row: replies (default) · messages · turns. The other 61 measures scroll right past guidance (/language all). ↑↓ turns · ←→ columns · ↵ opens the turn in the stream. (b) The session indicator: nothing until the detector signals; then ONE item at the end of the existing activity strip ("turn 33 · 18.4s · 1 tool · parked (replied) · high · full · blunt · words ↑"), several at once collapse to "N shifts"; hover/focus = two sentences per shift (value vs window in SDs + the setting in force); stays until the next setting change or /language clear; /language off|on; no clickable control. (c) Short sessions (the median): numbers and dots only; absent lines/bands explain themselves on hover ("the window needs 1,600 words; this session has 412"). (d) Explanations: one generated table (language_measures.gen.ts) feeds headers, indicator, drift marks and /help — dotted underline + help cursor, hover and focus, two sentences max, as the structure page does. Data: record events for values/drifts/changes; GET /api/session/<id>/language for windows, cached by last seq.
