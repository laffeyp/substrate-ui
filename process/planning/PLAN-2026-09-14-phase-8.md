# PLAN — Phase 8 · Session transparency + interrupt · 2026-09-14

## What this phase is

Phase 7 closed the flat-design parity for turn 19 and turn 20 at the
surface level. Phase 8 is the substance behind the tool call and
descent surfaces: every tool call becomes a legible, expandable row
with its args, output, and live streaming; ESC becomes a two-tier
interrupt that reaches the tool; descent reads the child record's
own envelopes; and everything scopes correctly when the user is
descended into a delegate.

## Design of record

`DESIGN-2026-09-14-r5-session-transparency.md`. The Architect's
turn-21 sheet ratifies the design as frames 21a through 21f.

## Items in order

Each item is a single work unit and stays green on its own.

1. **`session_controller.ts:698` carries args + output on the
   TranscriptRow.** Client only.
2. **`reveal.html` renders the tool card** via
   `S.toolOpen[call_id]` and the nine existing dc-runtime expand
   bindings. Client only.
3. **Live running seconds counter** on unpaired ToolCalls. Client
   only.
4. **Child-record reader on the controller.**
   `attachRecordRoot(record_root, sinceSeq)` on `SessionController`;
   a new `GET /api/records/by-path?path=<>&since_seq=<>` on the
   server; the descent click routes through it.
5. **`SessionRegistry.interrupt(target, tier)`** where target is
   `session_id` or `record_root`. Tier `"soft"` records
   `InterruptRequested`; tier `"hard"` walks `kind_by_instance` for
   both `"model"` and `"tool"` and cancels through
   `cancel_producer`. `server.py:1620` reads `tier` and, when
   descended, `record_root` from the query. Client wires two-press
   ESC.
6. **`InterruptRequested` envelope kind.** SDD vocabulary lock —
   `signals/versions/` bump, schema, kernel vocab entry.
7. **`interrupt_fragment_producer`** under `topologies/session/`,
   triggers on `InterruptRequested`, emits
   `PromptFragment(source="interrupt", …)`, folded by the existing
   `prompt_composer`.
8. **`ToolProgress` envelope + emission from `tools/runner.py`.**
   Shape `{ call_id, tool, step, chunk, offset, eof? }`, rate-limited
   1/250ms, size-capped 4KB. Client renders growing chunks in the
   open tool card and seals on `eof` or ToolResult.
9. **Descent-scope routing** in `reveal.ts`. The current descent
   stack per pane routes ESC to the correct target and the correct
   controller.

## Order of landing

1, 2, 3 land first — client-only, immediate legibility win. 4 wires
descent onto real child records; the depth-ramp accent from turn
20f now colors real content. 5 gives ESC a hard target at the
session level. 6 and 7 ship together — envelope and its subscriber
— giving ESC a soft target. 8 lands streaming into the tool cards
1–3 built. 9 closes the descent scope so ESC and the tool cards
work at any depth.

## What "done" looks like

- Every ToolCall row previews its first arg; the glyph carries
  state; the caret opens the args + output card.
- A running tool shows a live seconds counter and, when streaming,
  a growing output pane sealed on `eof`.
- First ESC on a running turn signals soft; the model returns after
  the current tool with a summary.
- Second ESC kills the tool; the failed ToolResult reaches the
  transcript in place.
- Descent onto a delegate shows the child's real tool rows, streams
  the child's tool progress, and scopes ESC to the child's runtime.
- A screenshot per frame (21a–21f) closes the phase.
