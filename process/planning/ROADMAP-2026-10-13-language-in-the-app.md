# ROADMAP: language measurements in the app

*2026-10-13. Design: `DESIGN-2026-10-13-language-in-the-app-r2.md`, as changed by the design sheet `design-sheets/2026-10-13-language/` (decisions D74–D80: the page is a stream of lanes in three views, numbers in one readout row; the bar line names its settings and puts each shift after the setting meant to move it). Review: `REVIEW-2026-10-13-language-designs.md`. Kernel: session vocabulary v0.11 § W, `substrate.language` 0.2.1.*

Seven cards, in order. Kernel cards in `substrate/process/sprints/`, UI cards in `substrate-ui/process/sprints/`.

| # | Card | Repo | What | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | K312 | substrate | **The measure table.** Every one of the 74 measures: short name, unit, group (length · tone · guidance · other), a typical display range, a two-sentence explanation; the intent table from the length and tone control texts (which measure each position states a direction for). One home in `substrate.language`. | Every profile measure has an entry; the 13 watched have their group; each intent cites the control text it comes from; a test fails on a measure without an entry. |
| 2 | K313 | substrate | **The language view.** `app.language_view(record)`: the command's lines plus, per stream and watched measure, the detector's warm-up state (the turn it ended, or not yet), its mean and SD, and each value in those SDs. One implementation of the detector, in the kernel. | On a built record, deviations equal (value − warm-up mean) / warm-up SD, warm-up ends where `PageHinkley` scales, marks equal the record's `LanguageDrift`. |
| 3 | U168 | substrate-ui | **The route and the generated table.** `GET /api/session/<id>/language` returns `language_view`, cached by the record's last seq; `scripts/gen_kinds.py` writes `web/vm/language_measures.gen.ts` from K312's table. | The route answers for a live session and an ended one; a second call within the same seq is served from the cache; the generated file is checked by the generated-kinds test. |
| 4 | U169 | substrate-ui | **The language lens, bars view.** A fifth meta tab after structure; lanes for the 13 measures in three groups; time down, 18 px rows; each turn an activation bar off the lane's baseline, the trailing window as a curve along the bar ends; the setting-change rule; shift marks; the readout row for the selected turn; streams replies · messages · turns; ↑↓ ←→ ↵; selection linked with stream+graph; hover explanations on every lane header and mark. | A gate drives a live session through a length change and reads the lens: lanes, rule, readout and hover text match the record. |
| 5 | U170 | substrate-ui | **The deviation view.** Every lane in warm-up SDs from K313, guides at ±2 SD, emphasis beyond them; the readout in signed SDs. | The gate reads deviations equal to the route's. |
| 6 | U171 | substrate-ui | **The ribbons view.** Each lane a ribbon whose width is the trailing window, each reply a hairline. | The gate reads ribbon widths proportional to the route's window values. |
| 7 | U172 | substrate-ui | **The bar line and `/language`.** Settings named (`guidance high · length full · tone blunt`); each shift as its measure and ↑/↓ after the setting meant to move it; hover opens one shift; several in one group collapse to a count; narrow panes wrap; `/language` opens the lens at the turn, `/language clear`, `/language off`, `/language on`. | A gate drives a shift and reads the line, the hover and each command; `controls_mid_session` reads the named form. |

Gates for every UI card run in `gates:electron`; the whole suite runs once at the end, with a signed build installed and checked on a real session.
