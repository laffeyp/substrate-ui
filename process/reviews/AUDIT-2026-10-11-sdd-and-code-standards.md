# AUDIT: SDD discipline and code standards, recent work

*2026-10-11. Asked by the Architect after a day of piecemeal work. Scope: every commit of 2026-10-10 and 2026-10-11 in both repos — kernel K280–K296, substrate-ui sprints 120–143 — measured against `sdd-kit-2/AGENTS.md` (twelve hard rules, the dual and observation contracts, the Rubber Duck Pass), `substrate/process/WORKING_AGREEMENT.md` and `substrate-ui/WORKING_AGREEMENT.md`, all three read in full for this audit. Findings only; each gives its evidence and what would restore the standard.*

## Verdict

The code is sound by its gates; the method around it has thinned. ruff, mypy `--strict`, tsc, lint-imports and the tone canon are clean on today's changes; no broad `except` was added; the behaviour gates were checked red on the old code; real-model checks ran. But since 2026-10-10 the sprint cards stopped carrying the kit's contracts, no sprint closed with a Rubber Duck Pass, four vocabulary rounds were signed only by the agent, and the new control names are retyped as strings in fourteen places. SDD is being followed in spirit (cards before code, tests, records), not in form, and the form is what the kit says carries across sessions.
